# Join repair: diagnose the pair before choosing the operation

A join repair is not automatically a transition, an extend or a generation. The accepted A → B cut
stays exactly as it is while the pair is diagnosed and candidates are compared.

## Evidence

Resolve the exact source files of both sides and any trims the user made. Look at real frames around the
boundary: take them with `picsart_scene_contact_sheet` and open them (picsart-video-rules, Asset URLs),
with enough of the action either side to follow the exchange, and say what you observed, with
source times:

- the movement's phase and direction; position, framing and eyelines;
- props, dialogue, room tone, emotional continuity;
- whether the incoming action is motivated by the outgoing beat;
- whether a black, frozen, flashing or repeated frame is in the source, in the join, or only in the
  player.

Name the failure plainly: overlap or dead time, a badly placed cut, an interrupted line,
incompatible staging, or missing story (an arrival nobody saw). Keep what you saw apart from what
you suspect. If you could not see something, say so; never guess. A deliberately abrupt cut is not
a defect.

## The smallest operation that fits

Offer only what the evidence supports, smallest first:

1. **Trim or move the cut**, for overlap, dead time or a bad cut point, applied once the user picks
   it. Keep whole words and needed action.
2. **A J or L cut of the existing sound**, for a gap or an abrupt answer, where the speaker's
   visibility, the lip-sync and the meaning allow. Never duplicate or invent a line.
3. **A crop or reframe**, only if the resolution and framing allow and the subject stays in frame.
   It cannot show an arrival nobody filmed.
4. **Existing coverage, or a motivated insert**, for a missing cause, arrival, reaction or answer.
   Use coverage you have before proposing a new shot with a clear story purpose.

Never a dissolve, a zoom or a fade as a generic cure for positions that disagree. Honour an
explicit "crop only", and say if a crop cannot solve what you saw. **Diagnosis is not permission to
spend.**
